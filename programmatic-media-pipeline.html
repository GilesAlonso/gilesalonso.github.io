Case Study

Generative Media Operations Platform

Distributed orchestration platform enabling a 40-year production company to become an Elite AI Partner operating across three continents.

The Context

A well-established production company with four decades of experience in corporate communication for major brands identified a strategic inflection point: generative AI could enable personalized video production at a scale previously impossible. The company's leadership—already experienced in spreadsheet automation and financial dashboards—envisioned serving multinational enterprise clients requiring point-of-sale-level personalization across North America, Latin America, and Europe.

The Operational Bottleneck

Delivering on this vision required orchestrating multiple generative AI APIs (video rendering and audio synthesis), each with strict rate limits, per-unit costs, and unpredictable latency. Manual coordination of thousands of rendering jobs across concurrent campaigns was impossible. The team needed complete visibility into production status, costs, and error handling—without investing in expensive custom infrastructure or training teams on unfamiliar tools across multiple regions.

The Systems Engineering Approach

We engineered a distributed operations platform built on Google Sheets—an intentional architectural choice prioritizing zero infrastructure cost, complete operational transparency, and leveraging the team's existing fluency with the tool across all operational regions.

  • Master-Satellite Architecture: Designed a hierarchical system where a master coordination sheet orchestrates production across multiple satellite sheets, each representing a distinct template or campaign. This enables parallel operation of concurrent campaigns across regions without interference.
  • Multi-API Orchestration with Intelligent Throttling: Built asynchronous queue management that coordinates calls across HeyGen (video generation) and ElevenLabs (audio synthesis), respecting each API's rate limits, handling retries, and maintaining production continuity even when individual services experience failures.
  • Webhook-Driven State Management: Implemented event-driven status updates where external services push completion events directly into the relational database, providing real-time visibility into thousands of concurrent jobs without resource-intensive polling.
  • Resilient Error Routing: Engineered automated error detection and isolation. Invalid payloads, API rejections, and data formatting issues are flagged and routed to dedicated error logs, allowing the production queue to bypass failures gracefully and continue processing the remaining batch autonomously.

The Outcome

The platform transformed the company's competitive positioning and technical capabilities. The system successfully supported national-scale advertising campaigns for major beverage brands, generating thousands of personalized videos—one for each point of sale across the country. Production capacity is no longer constrained by human coordination. The company now operates multiple concurrent enterprise campaigns across three continents, with complete operational transparency and zero infrastructure costs. This operational excellence contributed to the company achieving Elite Partner status with HeyGen, positioning them as a recognized leader in AI-powered video production. The platform enabled a 40-year production company to reinvent itself as a cutting-edge AI operations powerhouse, winning contracts that require personalization at multinational scale.
